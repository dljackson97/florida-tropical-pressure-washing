# Florida Tropical Pressure Washing — Sample Website

This is a **free sample website** built for Florida Tropical Pressure Washing by [Clementine Web Co.](https://clementinewebco.com), a small web design studio based in St. Petersburg, FL. It's not live yet and isn't an official Florida Tropical Pressure Washing website, just a preview of what one could look like.

## What's in this repo
A simple, framework-free, multi-page website:
- `index.html` — home
- `reviews.html` — real customer reviews pulled from Angi, plus links to the Angi and Yelp listings
- `gallery.html` — before/after by job type (placeholder cards pending real photos)
- `about.html` — the business's story, a DIY-vs-pro comparison, service area
- `services.html` — service breakdown and starting pricing
- `styles.css` — all styling
- `script.js` — nav, scroll reveal, and the one-time review popup

No build tools, no installs, open `index.html` in any browser to view it, or visit the live preview at **https://dljackson97.github.io/florida-tropical-pressure-washing/**.

## About the content
Business name, address, year established, and the 5.0 Angi rating shown here are real. The two customer quotes on the Reviews page are real, pulled directly from Angi (credited generically since no reviewer names were available). The before/after gallery is a placeholder, no real job photos exist publicly yet. Services pricing is illustrative starting-price ranges, not the business's confirmed real numbers.
